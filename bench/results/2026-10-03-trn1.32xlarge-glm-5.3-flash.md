## Speed (not yet tuned)

- Weights: 87 s per rank with a warm page cache; the first load from the 500 MB/s gp3 root
  volume took about 25 minutes (each rank reads every expert tensor's rows to cut its columns).
- Compile: decode (4, 4) 102 s, prefill (32, 4) 17 s (piecewise, layer graphs partly cached).
- Decode B=4: **267.5 ms per step, 15.0 tok/s** (p90 281.9 ms). Same shape as MiMo before the NKI
  MoE kernel (306 ms): one collective-bearing graph per MoE layer at ~5 ms each. Next: the NKI
  kernel with clamped SwiGLU and 128-block FP8 scales, then MoE layers grouped 12 per graph.

## Through the NKI MoE kernel, MoE layers 12 per graph (2026-10-03, engine-v0 35bf2c7)

Same instance and checkpoint, `KILN_MOE_KERNEL=nki KILN_PIECEWISE_MOE_GROUP=12` (the dedupe
kernel, kiln/kernels/moe_dedupe.py, with GLM's clamped SwiGLU and 128-block FP8 scales):

- `tools/check_ppl.py ... --tp 32 --piecewise --kv-cache-gb 1.0`: mean prompt logprob **-2.086**
  (XLA path -2.141; the per-sentence values move by 0.03-0.25 in both directions).
- `tools/check_device.py ... --max-num-seqs 4 --prefill-tokens 32 --bench-steps 16 --piecewise
  --kv-cache-gb 2.0`: decode B=4 **50.0 ms per step, 80.4 tok/s** (p90 51.5 ms), against 267.5 ms
  on the XLA path (5.4x). Greedy text coherent on all 8 prompts (Paris; a fibonacci generator;
  AWS; the Fibonacci series; Apollo 11; the sky).
- Compile: decode (4, 4) 761 s and prefill (32, 4) 983 s for the 12-layer group graphs, against
  102 s and 17 s with one graph per MoE layer: the price of few graphs is compile time, so sweeps
  pin their buckets (`bench/serve_sweep.py --decode-buckets ... --warmup`).
- Same, `--max-num-seqs 32 --kv-cache-gb 2.0`: decode B=32 **116.7 ms per step, 274.5 tok/s**
  (p90 117.8 ms); compile decode (32, 4) 958 s, and the prefill (32, 4) graph compiled again
  (986 s) because the KV pool shape changed with max_num_seqs.
