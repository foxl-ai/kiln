## Speed: W1, Qwen3-0.6B bf16

Workload `bench/offline.py`'s `workload()` (256 prompts of 100-1,024 random tokens, 100-1,024
output tokens each, 133,966 output tokens in total, temperature 0.6, ignore_eos); throughput =
output tokens / wall time after warm-up. Both engines on logical cores 8-11 (device 2, one
Trainium2), one job on the box at a time.

```
python bench/baseline_vllm.py --model Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B --tp 4 --core-base 8 --max-num-seqs N \
    --max-num-batched-tokens 2048 --max-model-len 2048            # [--tp 1 --dp 4 --max-num-seqs N/4]
python bench/offline.py --model Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B --tp 4 --core-base 8 --max-num-seqs N --decode-buckets N \
    --page-buckets 16,32,64 --prefill-buckets 512 --kv-cache-gb 6 --warmup --overlap   # [--dp 4 --tp 1]
```

vllm-neuron ran with its defaults otherwise: asynchronous scheduling on, block size 32,
`num_seqs_buckets` [1, 2, 4, ..., N], `num_batched_tokens_buckets` [128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048],
KV sized by itself (248,032 tokens at N=32), prefix caching off, `NEURON_SKIP_EFA_AFFINITY=1`.

| sequences in flight | vllm-neuron TP=4 | vllm-neuron DP=4 x TP=1 | Kiln TP=4 | Kiln DP=4 x TP=1 | best Kiln / best vllm-neuron |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 606.54 tok/s (220.87 s) | 308.56 tok/s (434.16 s) | **1,370.68** tok/s (97.74 s) | 1,177.05 tok/s (113.82 s) | 2.26x |
| 16 | 673.44 tok/s (198.93 s) | - | **2,089.63** tok/s (64.11 s) | - | 3.10x |
| 32 | 146.89 tok/s (912.01 s) | - | **1,621.70** tok/s (82.61 s) | - | 11.0x |

- vllm-neuron at 32 sequences is not a harness slip I could find: no recompile in the timed
  window, KV for 121 concurrent requests, and two variants were as slow or slower before I stopped
  them (`--max-num-batched-tokens 512`: 99 of 256 requests after 6.5 min; `--context-buckets
  512,1024,1536`, i.e. neuron_config `decode_context_length_buckets`: 12 of 256 after 3 min). Its
  throughput collapses between 16 and 32 sequences on this setup; the cause is not known.
- Kiln at 32 is below Kiln at 16 (1,621.70 against 2,089.63); not investigated yet.
- No compiles happened inside any timed Kiln run (`compiles during the timed run: none`).
- Engine start: vllm-neuron 105-197 s (compile cached after the first run); Kiln warm-up 288-343 s
  over 6 graphs, uncached.
