# MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL on trn1.32xlarge, tp=32 (2026-10-03)

Instance trn1.32xlarge (us-east-2c spot), Neuron SDK 2.32 DLAMI, model
`XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL` (309B total / 15B active, 48 layers, 256 experts), FP8 dense
weights (128x128 block scales), MXFP4 experts converted losslessly to FP8 with bf16 power-of-two
scales, bf16 KV, tensor parallelism 32 with vocab-parallel embedding and lm_head, piecewise
compilation with 6 layers per graph.

## Correctness

`python tools/check_ppl.py --model XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL --tp 32 --piecewise`:
mean prompt logprob **-2.161** nats per token over the four sentences in the tool (Qwen3-0.6B on
CPU: -2.038 on the same sentences). Before the two qkv_proj fixes below it was -9.75, and Xiaomi's
own modeling code fed the same (mis-read) weights layer by layer on the host
(`tools/ref_stream.py`) gave -9.65, i.e. the model code was right and the weight reading was not:

## Speed (not yet competitive)

- Weights: 101 s per rank (32 ranks in parallel, warm page cache), engine up 107 s.
- Compile (piecewise, cached layer graphs): decode (4, 4) 9.1 s, prefill (32, 4) 98.6 s.
- Decode batch 4: **571 ms per step (7 tok/s)**. Per 6-layer graph about 59 ms
  (`KILN_PROFILE_PIECES=1`), i.e. about 10 ms per layer, against 0.77 ms measured for one MoE
  layer at this rank shape in isolation (tools/profile_moe.py). Cause (found 2026-10-03 on
  trn1.2xlarge, docs/neuron-notes.md "MoE expert gathers"): with 6 MoE layers in one graph
  neuronx-cc splits the expert gathers into millions of tiny software-generated DMAs. Reproduced
  with two ranks at tp=32 shapes (550.4 ms per 48-layer step); one graph per MoE layer
  (perf/decode) took the same step to 55.3 ms. **Not yet re-measured at tp=32 on
  trn1.32xlarge.**
- No managed baseline exists for this model on Neuron (vllm-neuron 0.24 serves Llama, GPT-OSS,
  Qwen3 dense and Qwen3-VL only; see docs/research/trn2-trn3.md).

## Where the step goes at tp=32, and larger batches (2026-10-03, later)

`KILN_PROFILE_PIECES=1`, decode B=4: the 48 layer graphs take 5.8-6.5 ms each (12.0 ms for the
first), i.e. the whole 306 ms step, against 1.3 ms per MoE layer with two live ranks on one chip.
The difference is a fixed cost per execution of any graph holding a cross-chip collective (about
5 ms at 32 ranks, whatever the collective; docs/neuron-notes.md "Collectives across chips"), so
one graph per MoE layer pays it 48 times. Prefill C=32: 46 ms per MoE layer graph.

Larger decode batches (same command, `--max-num-seqs B --kv-cache-gb 2.0`, plain decode):

| B | step p50 | tokens/s | compile of the decode bucket |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 306 ms | 13.0 | 2.4 s (cached) |
| 16 | 1644 ms | 9.7 | 54 s |
| 32 | 2044 ms | 15.7 | 121 s |

Throughput does not scale with batch: at B=16 a MoE layer graph costs about 34 ms, the gathered
expert weights again lowered into small software DMAs (as at B=2 on trn1.2xlarge). Both costs
point at the same fix: an NKI expert kernel, so the compiler stops packetizing the gathers and
MoE layers can share graphs again (fewer collective-bearing executions per step).

## NKI decode MoE kernel, MoE layers grouped 12 per graph (2026-10-03, engine-v0 fd88fbd)

`KILN_MOE_KERNEL=nki KILN_PIECEWISE_MOE_GROUP=12`, otherwise the commands above
(`tools/check_device.py --model XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL --device neuron --tp 32
--piecewise ...`). The kernel (kiln/kernels/moe_decode.py) gathers whole expert blocks by one
dynamic DMA each, so the compiler no longer packetizes the gathers and 12 MoE layers share a
graph: 4 layer graphs per step instead of 48.

| mode | B | step p50 | tokens/s | before (one graph per MoE layer) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain | 4 | **32.3 ms** | **123.4** | 306 ms, 13.0 |
| plain | 16 | **56.4 ms** | **283.9** | 1644 ms, 9.7 |
| plain | 32 | **110.2 ms** | **291.0** | 2044 ms, 15.7 |
| MTP k=3 | 4 | 84.2 ms | 101.0 (2.12 tokens per sequence per step, 48% of 218 drafts) | 294 ms, 28.9 |

Text matches the run without the kernel on 7 of 8 prompts at B=4 (the Apollo prompt diverges
after 17 identical tokens, at a near tie); `tools/check_ppl.py --tp 32 --piecewise` mean
prompt logprob -2.174 against -2.16 without the kernel. Compile: decode (4, 4) 75 s, (16, 4)
103 s, (32, 4) 157 s; prefill (32, 4) 144-175 s; verify (4, 4, 4) 112 s.

MTP is now slower than plain decode: its three draft steps are three more collective-bearing
graph executions (about 5 ms fixed each at tp=32) on top of a verify step over 16 rows. Next:
unroll the k draft steps into one graph.

## MTP draft passes in one graph: A/B (2026-10-03, engine-v0 b0e5c2a against its parent)

Same box, interleaved runs, `KILN_MOE_KERNEL=nki KILN_PIECEWISE_MOE_GROUP=12 KILN_PROFILE_EXEC=1`
(every graph call timed synchronously), `--spec-method mtp --spec-k 3 --max-num-seqs 4
--bench-steps 32`:

| run | draft graphs per step | draft time | verify (4 x 4 rows) | step p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unfused, 1 | 3 | 3 x 10.8-11.5 ms | 59.1 ms | 93.2 ms |
| fused, 1 | 1 | 11.6 ms | 53.8 ms | 72.6 ms |
| unfused, 2 | 3 | 3 x 10.0-11.3 ms | 55.4 ms | 92.7 ms |
| fused, 2 | 1 | 13.1 ms | 63.4 ms | 80.7 ms |

Acceptance 47-48% (2.12 tokens per sequence per step) on both. Fusing saves 12-20 ms per step,
but MTP still loses to plain decode at B=4 (32.3 ms per step, 123 tok/s; MTP 105-117 tok/s):
the verify step over 16 rows reads 128 (token, expert) pairs per MoE layer against 32 for a
plain step, and the NKI kernel's cost follows the pairs (docs/neuron-notes.md). Plain decode is
the faster default for MoE models on trn1 at this batch; MTP pays off only where a step is
bound by something other than expert reads. Verify varies 54-63 ms between processes, so read
single runs with that spread in mind.
