# 2026-10-02, trn1.2xlarge (us-east-2c spot), Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B bf16

Workload: `bench/offline.py`'s `workload()`, identical to Mini-SGLang-Neuron's
`benchmark/offline/bench_vllm_neuron.py`: random.seed(0), 256 prompts of randint(100, 1024)
tokens, max_tokens randint(100, 1024) (133,966 output tokens), temperature 0.6, ignore_eos.
Throughput = output tokens / wall time after warmup. Both engines use both NeuronCores.

Stacks:
- vllm-neuron 0.5.3 (vLLM 0.16, NxD Inference 0.10, torch-neuronx 2.9, neuronx-cc 2.26),
  SDK 2.31.1 DLAMI `pytorch-inference-vllm-0.16`: `python bench/baseline_vllm.py --tp 2
  --max-num-seqs N --num-blocks M` (block_size 128, no prefix caching; Mini-SGLang-Neuron's
  settings at N=6, M=9).
- Kiln, SDK 2.32.0 DLAMI multi-framework (libtorch_neuronx_lite 2.11, neuronx-cc 2.27):
  `python bench/offline.py --dp 2 --max-num-seqs N/2 --decode-buckets N/2 --page-buckets
  16,32,64 --prefill-buckets 512 --kv-cache-gb 6 --warmup [--overlap]`.

## Equal total concurrency

| Concurrent sequences | vllm-neuron TP=2 | Kiln DP=2 | Kiln vs baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 380.45 tok/s | 380.36 tok/s (3 per replica, overlap, @ced8885) | parity |
| 12 | 570.49 tok/s (18 blocks) | 449.70 tok/s (6 per replica, no overlap, @0c968a4) | -21% |
| 32 | 817.95 tok/s (vllm-neuron sizes its own KV) | 690.49 tok/s (16 per replica, overlap, S3 compile cache, @cda7c08) | -16% |

Reading: parity at low concurrency, and the gap opens as the batch grows, because NxD
Inference's decode kernels scale with batch better than Kiln's torch-level attention and
per-layer small ops. The kernel work below is what closes it.
